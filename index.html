At Eaglenest Academy, we believe every child carries an extraordinary potential waiting to take flight. Our dedicated team of educators creates a safe, stimulating environment where learners are challenged to think critically, collaborate meaningfully, and grow holistically within the CBC framework.

The Eaglenest Difference

Why Families Choose Us

Six pillars that set Eaglenest apart and give every learner the best possible start.

CBC-Aligned Curriculum

Fully accredited under Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum with TSC-registered teachers trained in continuous assessment and portfolio evaluation.

85+ Qualified Educators

Degree-holding, TSC-registered teachers who receive ongoing professional development and are genuinely passionate about nurturing each learner holistically.

Modern Facilities

Smart classrooms, a well-stocked library, science and computer labs, an all-weather sports pitch, and a creative arts studio — within a secure, green campus.

Safe & Caring Environment

CCTV-monitored compound, licensed transport, a resident school counsellor, and a zero-tolerance anti-bullying policy ensure every child feels secure and valued.

Rich Co-curricular Life

Over 20 clubs and teams spanning drama, music, robotics, football, athletics, debate, and Scouts — developing well-rounded individuals beyond the classroom.

Strong Parent Partnership

A vibrant Parents Association, termly open days, real-time progress updates via our school portal, and an open-door policy — keeping families connected every step.
